\begin{abstract}
We study joint charging-infrastructure placement and energy-constrained multi-route planning for refrigerated battery-electric heavy trucks on a \textbf{national} Moroccan motorway envelope spanning Tangier--Rabat--Casablanca--F\`es--Oujda--Marrakech--Agadir.
The model couples an OSM/DEM physics stack, firm-ripe tomato refrigeration, multi-resource label setting (MRLS), and exhaustive site enumeration over direction-specific ADM service-area candidates.
Default CHARGE physics uses a conservative SOC-dependent CCS acceptance envelope
(\texttt{charging\_model=ccs\_soc\_taper\_research\_envelope}) --- a research assumption, not an OEM-measured eActros/Volvo curve --- replacing the retired constant-power planning default.
\textbf{Operational coverage} is the primary metric: continuous physics replay, action audit, d\'ecret~2-10-314 arts.~17--19 schedule validity, and speed-policy screens must all pass.

On a six-mission equal-weight national headline panel at energy quantum $q{=}2$~(kWh), joint multi-route enumeration (E) and sequential greedy (F) \emph{tie}: eActros reaches operational coverage $1.00$ at budget~B2 with directed Benguerir sites $\{y_{\mathrm{benguerir\_ac}},y_{\mathrm{benguerir\_ca}}\}$, versus $0.83$ for optimized fixed-route planning (C) --- an E$-$C gap of one mission ($+1/6$).
Volvo FH saturates at $0.83$ (E$=$F) with $\{y_{\mathrm{benguerir\_ca}},y_{\mathrm{bouznika\_rc}},y_{\mathrm{settat\_ca}}\}$ and retains a larger E$-$C gap ($+2/6$ at Ball).
Tangier$\rightarrow$Casablanca and Casa$\rightarrow$Rabat / Rabat$\rightarrow$F\`es legs are DEM-complete and operational under the same physics; east Oujda path unions remain incomplete where COP-DEM sampling failed quality gates, and no invented ADM attach is used where Msoun/La\^ayoune lack \texttt{entrance\_and\_exit}.
Chargers are hypothetical HGV CCS posts; costs and OD weights are research proxies, not observed freight counts.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:intro}

Battery-electric heavy-duty tractors are discussed for Moroccan long-distance logistics, including temperature-controlled export chains from Souss-Massa toward northern and eastern gateways.
This paper asks how much \emph{operational} freight service joint charger placement and energy-aware multi-route planning unlock when missions must obey DEM physics, SOC-dependent CCS taper, speed policy, and professional-driver pause rules on a national motorway spine --- not a single southern corridor alone.

\paragraph{Non-claims.}
We do not assert a new joint location--routing formulation or OEM-calibrated charge curves.
The CCS taper is a transparent research envelope.
We do not recommend specific ADM investments, and equal mission weights are not observed OD volumes.

\paragraph{Contributions.}
\begin{enumerate}
  \item Operational coverage vs budget under SOC CCS research taper on a national OD set with topology-verified ADM candidates.
  \item Mechanism-separated evidence that route flexibility and charger location change executable missions; E and F tie at eActros full coverage with two Benguerir directed sites.
  \item Honest geospatial gates: DEM-complete ODs only; no invented northern/eastern charger attach where ADM$\times$OSM access fails.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Methods (summary)}
\label{sec:methods}

Path unions are built from verified OSM alternatives ($max\_alts{=}2$) on a national bbox.
DRIVE uses COP-DEM profiles when \texttt{dem\_complete}; CHARGE duration integrates
\texttt{nameplate}\(\times\eta\times\)\texttt{conservative\_ccs\_taper\_factor(SOC)}.
Stations keep nameplate kW; taper applies at charge application.
Independent ADM rest pads validate schedules when energy-optimal itineraries skip charging.

\section{Limitations}
\label{sec:limits}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Taper is a research envelope, not OEM-measured.
  \item No observed refrigerated eHDT trip sample.
  \item East Oujda DEM residual / unmatched Msoun--La\^ayoune: no invented chargers.
  \item Headline enumeration uses a five-site tractable candidate set; Assilah/Larache ladders appear in frontier/audit configs.
\end{itemize}

\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}
Under SOC-dependent CCS research taper on a national Moroccan motorway panel, joint multi-route siting and greedy siting \emph{tie} at full eActros operational coverage with two Benguerir directed sites, while optimized fixed routing loses one mission.
National northern legs are feasible under the same evidence classes; eastern long-haul claims wait on complete DEM and ADM attach.
