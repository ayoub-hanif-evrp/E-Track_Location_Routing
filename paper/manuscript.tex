\title{Operational joint charging placement for refrigerated electric heavy-duty trucks on Moroccan motorways: HOS-in-search, directional access, and energy-discretization effects}
\author{Ayoub Hanif}
\address{EMSI / PhD research, Casablanca, Morocco}
\begin{abstract}
We study joint charger placement and energy-feasible routing for refrigerated electric heavy-duty trucks (eHDT) on Morocco's motorway spine.
The contribution is not a new general-purpose location--routing algorithm, but a mechanism-focused operational pipeline:
DEM-aware physics, direction-sensitive ADM candidate chargers, and hours-of-service (HOS) constraints enforced \emph{inside} label-setting search.
On a frozen 14-mission equal-weight panel with nine directional candidates, exhaustive subset enumeration under cardinality $\le 2$
(with 45~s screening and 600~s winner re-evaluation) yields a certified best-known joint multi-route gain for the eActros~600 at budget B1
($E{=}0.786$ vs fixed $C{=}0.714$; $+1/14$ missions), while B2 saturates at $E{=}C{=}0.786$.
Volvo coverage is flat ($E{=}C{=}F{=}0.571$).
Validation shows that apparent fixed$\subseteq$multi flexibility losses at 500k expansions were \texttt{LIMIT\_REACHED} artifacts;
at $2\times10^{6}$ expansions nesting is restored.
The same Benguerir install matches $E$ coverage under a shared final evaluator once LIMITs are escalated.
A controlled $q$-grid experiment on Agadir$\rightarrow$Casablanca (15~t, SOC~$0.8$) shows coarse $q{=}5$ false infeasibility relative to $q\in\{2,1\}$
(near-reserve min SOC $\approx 10.8\%$).
Claims are tied to Stage~C CSVs, \texttt{SCIENTIFIC\_VALIDATION\_REPORT.md}, and \texttt{campaign\_manifest.json}.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:intro}
Electrifying refrigerated long-haul freight requires co-design of infrastructure and operationally feasible itineraries under energy, time, and HOS limits.
We scope a Moroccan motorway case with directional service-area candidates and report only claims that survive matched solver certification.
Related recent TR-C work on e-truck charging and HOS~\cite{VidottePlaza2026TRC,Cheng2026TemperatureAware} motivates our focus on
operational feasibility boundaries rather than a new generic MIP LRP.

\section{Methods}
\label{sec:methods}
MRLS label setting with HOS-in-search resources (continuous drive clock reset by $\ge 45$~min consecutive CHARGE+WAIT;
daily driving hard cap without overnight Art.~16 reset) produces operational coverage after discrete/continuous replay and schedule audit.
Placement uses cardinality-capped enumeration (E) and greedy-over-enum scores (F; not an independent compute baseline).
Matched ablation re-evaluates fixed/multi masks on identical site sets at 600~s / $2\times10^{6}$ expansions for certification.
Energy-only dual solves are reported separately from HOS-ops coverage.

\section{Case design}
\label{sec:case}
Fourteen DEM-complete ODs~\cite{OpenStreetMapMoroccoMotorway,CopernicusDEMGLO30}; weather caches~\cite{OpenMeteoWeather};
nine grounded directional ADM sites; headline payload/SOC mix with southbound stress SOC~$0.5$ on selected ODs;
primary $q{=}2$~kWh with controlled $q\in\{5,2,1\}$ experiments.
Headline enum screen: 45~s; winners and certified ablation: 600~s (ablation expansions $2\times10^{6}$).

\section{Results}
\label{sec:results}

\subsection{Headline coverage (best-known under cardinality $\le 2$)}
Pipeline \texttt{hos\_in\_search\_enum\_cap2\_screen45\_final600} --- not a full $2^{9}$ optimum.
eActros B1: $C{=}0.714$, $E{=}F{=}0.786$ ($\Delta E{-}C{=}0.071$); B2--Ball: $C{=}E{=}F{=}0.786$.
Volvo B2: $C{=}E{=}F{=}0.571$.
Evidence: \texttt{q2\_critical\_portfolio.csv}.

\subsection{Route nesting and placement vs flexibility}
Published fixed$>$multi ops losses on C at 500k expansions were \texttt{LIMIT\_REACHED} artifacts
(\texttt{casa\_agadir}, \texttt{casa\_tangier}).
At $2\times10^{6}$ expansions both ODs restore \texttt{BOTH\_OPS} on C-site corners
(\texttt{results/validation/p1\_nesting/}).
We withdraw certified flexibility-\emph{loss} claims based on incomplete solves.

\subsection{Fair site evaluation (blind vs enum)}
Independent blind greedy and enum both select \texttt{y\_benguerir\_ca} at B2.
Under a shared aggregator, the frozen Benguerir install matches $E{=}0.786$ after escalating a single
\texttt{LIMIT\_REACHED} OD (\texttt{results/validation/p2\_fair\_eval/}).
Prior $10/14$ vs $11/14$ was not evidence that enumeration outperforms greedy on identical installs.

\subsection{Energy discretization}
Controlled protocol (Agadir$\rightarrow$Casablanca, 15~t, SOC~$0.8$, \texttt{y\_benguerir\_ac}):
$q{=}5$ is \texttt{INFEASIBLE}; $q\in\{2,1\}$ are \texttt{OPTIMAL} with min SOC $\approx 0.108$
(\texttt{results/validation/p5\_qgrid/}).
This is a coarse-grid false infeasibility, not a timeout.

\subsection{Southbound sensitivity}
With Benguerir CA, Casa$\rightarrow$Marrakech / Casa$\rightarrow$Agadir remain feasible for SOC$\ge 0.6$ across
$\{5.3,10,15\}$~t; failures concentrate at SOC~$0.5$ with heavier payloads
(\texttt{results/validation/p6\_sensitivity/}).
These are model-scenario fractions, not calibrated real-world probabilities.

\subsection{HOS scope}
HOS-in-search enforces continuous and daily driving resources; overnight daily-rest reset is \emph{not} implemented.
Ops-true mini-panel rows are audit-only with zero post-hoc pads (\texttt{hos\_mini\_panel\_gate.csv}).

\section{Discussion}
\label{sec:disc}
Two defensible contributions survive validation:
\textbf{(A)} infrastructure substitution --- flexible routing and directional placement can achieve the same operational coverage with fewer sites under stated limits;
\textbf{(B)} numerical feasibility --- energy quantization, directional access, and operational resources materially change model-based feasibility conclusions.
We do not claim a new general LRP solver~\cite{VidottePlaza2026TRC,Cheng2026TemperatureAware,Bank2025IJR}.

\section{Limitations}
\label{sec:limits}
Cardinality cap~$2$ with 45~s screening; B3/Ball are best-of-cache plus selected evaluations.
Matched ablation certification uses $2\times10^{6}$ expansions because 500k induces LIMIT artifacts on some multi-mask cells.
No Art.~16 overnight daily-rest reset; no observed eHDT trip sample; CCS taper is a research envelope.
CI graph tests require the packed multiroute artifact (\texttt{GRAPH\_ARTIFACT\_URL}).

\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}
Operational HOS-in-search, directional ADM access, and energy discretization change which refrigerated eHDT missions appear feasible
on Morocco's motorway spine.
Validated evidence supports modest certified joint-siting gains under cardinality caps, restored nesting after LIMIT escalation,
and a clear coarse-grid false-infeasibility case --- with honest non-gains for Volvo and higher budgets.
