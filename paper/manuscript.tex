\begin{abstract}
We study which refrigerated battery-electric heavy-duty truck (eHDT) missions on a national Moroccan motorway envelope
(Tangier--Rabat--Casablanca--F\`es--Marrakech--Agadir) remain \emph{operationally} feasible once (i)~Moroccan
hours-of-service (HOS) continuous and daily driving limits, (ii)~direction-specific Autoroutes du Maroc (ADM)
service-area access, and (iii)~energy quantum resolution enter a multi-resource label-setting (MRLS) search.
The stack couples OSM/DEM physics, firm-ripe tomato refrigeration, SOC-dependent CCS acceptance
(\texttt{charging\_model=ccs\_soc\_taper\_research\_envelope}; research assumption, not OEM-measured), and
exhaustive or matched placement evaluation over grounded directional candidates.
\textbf{Operational coverage} requires an energy-feasible itinerary under continuous physics replay \emph{and}
an HOS-legal schedule; search embeds continuous-driving and daily-driving resources so legality is optimized
rather than repaired with post-hoc pads.
We always record a separate energy-only solve so energy coverage is never conflated with operational coverage.
Authoritative numerics live under \texttt{results/paper\_campaign/} with pipeline tag
\texttt{hos\_in\_search\_full\_enum} (or documented certified lower bounds when \texttt{LIMIT\_REACHED} remains).
Joint fixed/multi placement comparisons (methods C/E/F) are narrated only when solver status is certified and
timeouts match across corners; unresolved cells are counted separately, never as infeasible.
Chargers are hypothetical; OD weights are equal research proxies.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:intro}

Battery-electric heavy-duty tractors are discussed for Moroccan long-distance refrigerated logistics.
Related TR-C work already combines charger location with routing and driver hours~\cite{VidottePlaza2026TRC}
and studies temperature-aware national siting and sizing~\cite{Cheng2026TemperatureAware}.
Our contribution is \emph{mechanism}-oriented and case-grounded: we show that operational constraints
(HOS continuous + daily driving under d\'ecret~2-10-314), directional ADM access geometry, and
energy-grid resolution materially change which refrigerated eHDT missions appear feasible on a
DEM-complete Moroccan motorway spine~\cite{OpenStreetMapMoroccoMotorway,CopernicusDEMGLO30,ADMServiceAreas,Bank2025IJR}.

\paragraph{Locked contribution.}
We do \emph{not} claim a new generic location--routing theorem, OEM-calibrated charge curves, measured traffic
weights, or certified 350~kW truck chargers at ADM sites.
Joint C/E/F ``multi-route coverage gains'' are reported only after identical solver settings and certified status.

\paragraph{Non-claims.}
No observed eHDT trip sample; axle/HGV access unverified; eastern Oujda ODs excluded without invented attach.

\section{Mathematical model}
\label{sec:model}

\subsection{Routing resources and HOS-in-search}
A label is
\[
L=(i,t,E^{\mathrm{tr}},E^{\mathrm{tl}},c,\kappa,\delta_{\mathrm{c}},\delta_{\mathrm{d}},\sigma),
\]
where $i$ is the node, $t$ discrete time, $E$ on-grid tractor/trailer energies, $c$ en-route cost,
$\kappa\in\{0,1\}$ charge eligibility, $\delta_{\mathrm{c}}$ continuous-driving steps since the last
qualifying break, $\delta_{\mathrm{d}}$ daily-driving steps, and $\sigma$ stationary accumulation used to
recognize consecutive CHARGE+WAIT at the same rest-capable node as one break (matching the operational
schedule auditor, including art.~19 split-break logic when enabled).

DRIVE accumulates $\delta_{\mathrm{c}}$ and $\delta_{\mathrm{d}}$; expansions that would exceed the continuous
cap (4.5~h) or the daily cap (9~h, d\'ecret art.~17) are forbidden under HOS-in-search~\cite{MoroccoDecret210314}.
Qualifying rest (CHARGE and/or WAIT totaling $\ge$45~min at rest-capable stations) resets $\delta_{\mathrm{c}}$;
daily reset follows the same qualifying daily-rest policy already used by schedule validators
(documented in code: overnight/daily rest at rest-capable nodes).
Dominance is same-$(i,t)$ Pareto over $(c,E,\kappa,\delta_{\mathrm{c}},\delta_{\mathrm{d}})$ with smaller
HOS resources weakly preferred.

\subsection{Energy-only versus operational solves}
For every mission--installation pair we run two MRLS solves: (i)~energy-only
(\texttt{hos\_in\_search=False}) and (ii)~HOS-ops (\texttt{hos\_in\_search=True}).
\texttt{operationally\_served} requires OPTIMAL HOS-ops status, continuous replay, schedule audit with
\texttt{daily\_driving\_ok}, and \texttt{hos\_search\_audit\_only} (zero post-hoc pads).
\texttt{LIMIT\_REACHED} is unresolved, not unserved.

\subsection{Placement}
For candidate sites $Y$ and budgets $B$, exhaustive enumeration maximizes equal-weight operational coverage
over missions $M$, with secondary cost tie-breaks.
Method C optimizes fixed-route masks; E optimizes multi-route masks; F is greedy over \emph{precomputed}
exhaustive subset scores unless an independent blind greedy matches E
(\texttt{blind\_greedy\_vs\_enum.csv}, engine \texttt{blind\_greedy\_live\_eval}).

\paragraph{Complexity note.}
With $|Y|=9$ directional sites, exhaustive subset evaluation is $2^{|Y|}$ multi-route portfolio solves,
each mission dual-solved; wall-clock is dominated by long ODs under HOS branching. We therefore report
certified coverage and unresolved counts explicitly.

\section{Algorithms}
\label{sec:algo}
MRLS expands DRIVE/CHARGE/WAIT with state-dependent drive tables from the physics adapter.
HOS-in-search prunes illegal driving expansions and resets break clocks via stationary accumulation so that
a 20+25~min CHARGE+WAIT pair at one node is one break in search, matching audit.
Placement uses exhaustive enum (E) and greedy-over-enum (F); matched ablation re-evaluates fixed/multi masks
on identical C-sites and E-sites at the same \texttt{max\_seconds}.

\section{Case design}
\label{sec:case}
National DEM-complete ODs~\cite{OpenStreetMapMoroccoMotorway,CopernicusDEMGLO30}; weather caches~\cite{OpenMeteoWeather};
nine grounded directional ADM sites; payloads $\{5.3,10,15\}$~t; SOC $\{0.5,0.8,1.0\}$; primary $q{=}2$~kWh
(with $q{=}1$ boundary refinement and optional $q{=}5$ companion).
Uniform solver limits for headline enum and matched ablation: 600~s / 500k expansions
(\texttt{multiroute\_placement\_corridor\_portfolio.yaml}).

\section{Discussion and related work}
\label{sec:disc}
Compared with~\cite{VidottePlaza2026TRC}, we emphasize Moroccan DEM/ADM geometry, refrigerated loads, and
numerical feasibility boundaries rather than a new hours-integrated MIP.
Compared with~\cite{Cheng2026TemperatureAware}, we focus on refrigerated TRU + DEM physics and
\emph{operational} HOS-in-search on a directional ADM candidate set, not national temperature-equilibrium
siting/sizing.
Equal weights and research taper limit external validity~\cite{Bank2025IJR}.
HOS-in-search lowers headline coverage relative to energy-only because long ODs hit expansion limits;
unresolved cells are labeled \texttt{LIMIT\_REACHED}, not physical impossibility.

\section{Limitations}
\label{sec:limits}
Enum cache scoring uses 45~s with cardinality cap~$2$ (exact for B0--B2); winners are re-evaluated at 600~s with energy-only dual solves. B3/Ball optimality is relative to that cache plus the all-sites evaluation.
A full two-vehicle payload$\times$SOC$\times$infra frontier under HOS is computationally heavy; we report
certified headline/ablation/q1 evidence and treat broad frontier/robustness grids as optional appendix work.
Some long ODs may remain \texttt{LIMIT\_REACHED} under screens; they are not counted as certified infeasible.
No observed eHDT trip sample; axle/HGV access unverified; eastern Oujda ODs excluded without invented attach.
CCS taper is a research envelope, not an OEM curve.

\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}
Operational constraints, directional ADM access, and energy discretization change which refrigerated eHDT
missions appear feasible on Morocco's motorway spine.
The repository separates energy-only from HOS-ops coverage, certifies served itineraries under audit-only
replay, and reports joint placement comparisons only when certified.
Claims are tied to Stage Gate CSVs and \texttt{campaign\_manifest.json}.
