\begin{abstract}
We study joint charging-infrastructure placement and energy-constrained multi-route planning for refrigerated battery-electric heavy trucks on a \textbf{national} Moroccan motorway envelope spanning Tangier--Rabat--Casablanca--F\`es--Marrakech--Agadir.
The model couples an OSM/DEM physics stack, firm-ripe tomato refrigeration, multi-resource label setting (MRLS), and exhaustive site enumeration over direction-specific ADM service-area candidates.
Default CHARGE physics uses a conservative SOC-dependent CCS acceptance envelope
(\texttt{charging\_model=ccs\_soc\_taper\_research\_envelope}) --- a research assumption, not an OEM-measured eActros/Volvo curve --- replacing the retired constant-power planning default.
\textbf{Operational coverage} is the primary metric: continuous physics replay, action audit, d\'ecret~2-10-314 arts.~17--19 schedule validity, and speed-policy screens must all pass.

On a fourteen-mission equal-weight national headline panel (all DEM-complete ODs) at energy quantum $q{=}2$~(kWh) with the full nine-site grounded candidate set, joint multi-route enumeration (E) and sequential greedy (F) \emph{tie} at every budget: eActros reaches operational coverage $0.86$ at budget~B2 with $\{y_{\mathrm{benguerir\_ca}},y_{\mathrm{bouznika\_cr}}\}$ (12/14 missions) versus $0.79$ for optimized fixed-route planning (C; 11/14) --- an E$-$C gap of $+0.07$.
At Ball, eActros E$=$F$=0.93$ vs C$=0.86$.
Volvo FH at B2 ties E$=$F$=$C$=0.64$ (9/14); the E$-$C gap appears only at Ball ($0.86$ vs $0.79$).
A full payload$\times$SOC frontier (5.3/10/15~t $\times$ SOC 0.5/0.8/1.0 across 14 ODs; 684 screen cells + 210 decisive $q{=}2$ confirms) shows flexibility ops-gains concentrated on a small Volvo subset, while Agadir$\rightarrow$Casablanca at 15~t remains unserved at 80\% departure SOC across AC ladders and becomes operational at 100\% SOC.
Chargers are hypothetical HGV CCS posts; costs and OD weights are research proxies, not observed freight counts.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:intro}

Battery-electric heavy-duty tractors are discussed for Moroccan long-distance logistics, including temperature-controlled export chains from Souss-Massa toward northern gateways.
This paper asks how much \emph{operational} freight service joint charger placement and energy-aware multi-route planning unlock when missions must obey DEM physics, SOC-dependent CCS taper, speed policy, and professional-driver pause rules on a national motorway spine --- not a single southern corridor alone.

\paragraph{Non-claims.}
We do not assert a new joint location--routing formulation or OEM-calibrated charge curves.
The CCS taper is a transparent research envelope.
We do not recommend specific ADM investments, and equal mission weights are not observed OD volumes.

\paragraph{Contributions.}
\begin{enumerate}
  \item Operational coverage vs budget under SOC CCS research taper on a national 14-OD DEM-complete panel with nine topology-verified ADM candidates.
  \item Mechanism-separated evidence that route flexibility and charger location change executable missions; E and F tie across the full budget ladder, with a modest eActros E$-$C gap ($+1/14$ at B2--Ball).
  \item Honest geospatial gates: DEM-complete ODs only; no invented charger attach where ADM$\times$OSM access fails.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Methods (summary)}
\label{sec:methods}

Path unions are built from verified OSM alternatives ($max\_alts{=}2$) on a national bbox.
DRIVE uses COP-DEM profiles when \texttt{dem\_complete}; CHARGE duration integrates
\texttt{nameplate}\(\times\eta\times\)\texttt{conservative\_ccs\_taper\_factor(SOC)}.
Stations keep nameplate kW; taper applies at charge application.
Independent ADM rest pads validate schedules when energy-optimal itineraries skip charging.
Headline portfolios enumerate the full nine grounded sites; payload$\times$SOC frontier and moderate robustness cells reuse the same physics stack.

\section{Limitations}
\label{sec:limits}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Taper is a research envelope, not OEM-measured.
  \item No observed refrigerated eHDT trip sample.
  \item Equal / illustrative OD weights $\neq$ traffic sample; axle-law and HGV access at ADM sites unverified.
  \item Eastern long-haul (Oujda) and unmatched Msoun--La\^ayoune attaches remain outside the DEM-complete panel --- no invented chargers.
\end{itemize}

\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}
Under SOC-dependent CCS research taper on a national Moroccan motorway panel, joint multi-route siting and greedy siting \emph{tie} across budgets: eActros reaches $0.86$ operational coverage at B2 ($12/14$) with Benguerir-CA and Bouznika-CR, while optimized fixed routing loses one mission ($11/14$).
Heavy-payload northbound Agadir$\rightarrow$Casablanca remains an operating-envelope finding (needs near-full departure SOC), not a solver artifact.
National northern legs are feasible under the same evidence classes; eastern long-haul claims wait on complete DEM and ADM attach.
