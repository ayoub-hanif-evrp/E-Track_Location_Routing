\title{Operational Feasibility and Charging Infrastructure Requirements for Battery-Electric Refrigerated Freight: A Scenario-Based Assessment of Moroccan Motorway Corridors}
\author{Ayoub Hanif}
\address{EMSI / PhD research, Casablanca, Morocco}
\begin{abstract}
We assess under which vehicle, infrastructure, and operating conditions battery-electric refrigerated heavy-duty trucks can feasibly serve selected Moroccan motorway freight missions within a single duty period.
A multi-resource label-setting solver with hours-of-service (HOS) resources enforced inside search is used as a research instrument, not as a claimed algorithmic contribution.
On a frozen 14-mission panel, matched multi-route re-evaluation at $600$~s / $2\times10^{6}$ expansions yields an observed infrastructure ladder of $9/14$ with no new chargers, $11/14$ with a southbound Benguerir site, and $12/14$ with Benguerir plus Larache ($0$ unresolved \texttt{LIMIT\_REACHED}).
A historical cardinality-capped enum selection that installed only Benguerir remains $11/14$ and is reported separately as an algorithm-selection result---not as the best observed two-site coverage.
Research questions address operating thresholds, directional site budgets, and sensitivity to routing flexibility, energy discretization, refrigeration assumptions, and physical parameters.
Cold-chain parameters follow UNECE firm-ripe tomato guidance (setpoint mid-band $9$~$^{\circ}$C), not a generic $+2$~$^{\circ}$C chiller claim.
Claims are bound to Path~A validation tables with solver statuses; \texttt{LIMIT\_REACHED} is never equated with infeasibility.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:intro}
Electrifying refrigerated long-haul freight requires answering an operational question before algorithm novelty:
under which vehicle, infrastructure, and duty conditions can missions be served within a single duty period?
Recent TR-C work on e-truck charging and HOS~\cite{VidottePlaza2026TRC,Cheng2026TemperatureAware} motivates studying feasibility boundaries rather than competing on a new generic location--routing formulation.
This paper reports a scenario-based assessment of Moroccan motorway corridors.
The solver (MRLS with HOS-in-search) is an instrument for that assessment.

\section{Research questions}
\label{sec:rq}
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{RQ1.} Which payload / departure-SOC thresholds separate feasible from infeasible refrigerated eHDT missions?
\item \textbf{RQ2.} How does operational coverage change for the observed infrastructure configurations none $\rightarrow$ Benguerir $\rightarrow$ Benguerir+Larache?
\item \textbf{RQ3.} How sensitive are conclusions to routing flexibility, energy discretization~$q$, refrigeration demand, and physical assumptions?
\end{enumerate}

\section{Methods}
\label{sec:methods}
\subsection{Instrument and HOS scope}
DEM-aware traction, auxiliaries, and an approximate refrigeration (UA--COP) load feed discrete DRIVE tables.
Directional ADM service-area candidates may be activated as chargers.
MRLS enforces continuous driving (qualifying break $\ge 45$~min from consecutive CHARGE+WAIT at the same rest node) and Art.~17 daily driving ($\le 9$~h) inside search.
Overnight Art.~16 daily-rest reset is not implemented; missions requiring it are labeled \texttt{MULTI\_DAY\_OUT\_OF\_SCOPE} and excluded from served counts
(\texttt{results/validation/path\_a/hos\_single\_duty\_policy.md}).
Independent schedule audit must agree with search break logic.
Energy-only coverage uses a separate \texttt{hos\_in\_search=False} solve.

\subsection{Case design}
Fourteen equal-weight ODs~\cite{OpenStreetMapMoroccoMotorway,CopernicusDEMGLO30}; weather caches~\cite{OpenMeteoWeather};
nine grounded directional ADM sites; primary $q{=}2$~kWh; certification protocol $600$~s / $2\times10^{6}$ expansions with
status $\in\{\texttt{OPTIMAL},\texttt{INFEASIBLE},\texttt{LIMIT\_REACHED}\}$.

\subsection{Cold-chain assumptions}
The headline commodity is firm-ripe fresh tomatoes.
The campaign refrigeration YAML uses setpoint $9$~$^{\circ}$C (UNECE ECE/TRADE/470 band $8$--$10$~$^{\circ}$C)~\cite{UNECETomato2023}.
A legacy $+2$~$^{\circ}$C approximation file is retained only for sensitivity contrast and is not the commodity claim
(\texttt{results/validation/path\_a/coldchain/}).

\subsection{Physics plausibility}
Flat-cruise traction for a representative refrigerated combination is order $1.34$~kWh/km versus a published eActros long-haul band $\sim 1.11$--$1.14$~kWh/km (different duty cycles).
Reference checks are tabulated in \texttt{physics\_plausibility.csv}; we claim order-of-magnitude credibility, not field validation.

\section{Results}
\label{sec:results}

\subsection{RQ2 --- observed infrastructure ladder}
Matched multi-route evaluation
(\texttt{results/validation/path\_a/infra\_ladder/}):
\begin{itemize}
\item I0 (no sites): $9/14$ ops, $0$ LIMIT;
\item I1 (Benguerir CA): $11/14$ ops, $0$ LIMIT;
\item I2 (Benguerir CA + Larache): $12/14$ ops, $0$ LIMIT.
\end{itemize}
The additional I2 mission relative to I1 is \texttt{casa\_tangier} (Larache direction unlocks service).
Historical screened enum selection $E$ that installs only Benguerir remains $11/14$ and is preserved as an algorithm-selection table---not as best-known two-site coverage.

\subsection{RQ1 --- operating thresholds}
With Benguerir CA, southbound Casa$\rightarrow$Marrakech / Casa$\rightarrow$Agadir remain largely feasible for departure SOC~$\ge 0.6$ across tested payloads;
failures concentrate at SOC~$0.5$ with heavier loads
(\texttt{results/validation/p6\_sensitivity/}).
These are model-scenario fractions, not calibrated probabilities.

\subsection{Energy versus operational coverage}
On conventional fixed-route Settat installs (B1/B2), mission records show \texttt{casa\_agadir} returning \texttt{LIMIT\_REACHED} under HOS-on search while the energy-only solve is \texttt{OPTIMAL}.
That gap is unresolved computation---not certified HOS or physical infeasibility
(\texttt{energy\_ops\_limit\_contamination.csv}).
Conventional $B$ rows use fixed routing with Settat-first sites; they are not a ``no-site baseline'' except at B0.

\subsection{RQ3 --- flexibility, discretization, refrigeration, physics}
Matched ablation multi-on-C equals the observed I2 ladder ($12/14$).
Controlled Agadir$\rightarrow$Casablanca (15~t, SOC~$0.8$): $q{=}5$ is \texttt{INFEASIBLE} while $q\in\{2,1\}$ are \texttt{OPTIMAL} near reserve --- coarse-grid false infeasibility.
Refrigeration-on versus off and UA/setpoint band variants re-bake DRIVE tables before evaluation.
On Benguerir-served Casa$\rightarrow$Marrakech / Casa$\rightarrow$Agadir (15~t, SOC~$0.8$), ops feasibility is unchanged across on/off and UA$\times 1.25$;
min SOC shifts only by $\sim 10^{-3}$ (larger near-reserve Agadir cell), so refrigerated freight is distinguishable in energy reserve but not always in binary coverage
(\texttt{coldchain/coldchain\_variants.csv}).
CdA/aux/weather/charger-power sensitivities re-bake DRIVE tables and change min SOC
(e.g.\ CdA$\times 1.1$ lowers Casa$\rightarrow$Marrakech min SOC from $0.284$ to $0.271$).
Charger power is varied in prepare ($250$ vs $350$~kW); on the near-reserve Agadir cell, $250$~kW yields a slightly lower min SOC than $350$~kW ($0.121$ vs $0.122$), confirming the override is physical rather than a label-only stub
(\texttt{results/validation/p6\_extras/southbound\_extras.csv}).

\section{Discussion}
\label{sec:disc}
Reporting a better-evaluated two-site configuration ($12/14$) alongside a screened one-site selection ($11/14$) is required for honesty; zero further gain would also have been a legitimate finding.
Plaza et al.\ and temperature-aware TR-C work~\cite{VidottePlaza2026TRC,Cheng2026TemperatureAware} remain motivation for not competing on algorithm novelty.
Consistency checks flag better-evaluated portfolios and \texttt{LIMIT}-contaminated energy/ops gaps
(\texttt{results/validation/consistency\_report.json}).

\section{Limitations}
\label{sec:limits}
Single duty day (no Art.~16 overnight reset); cardinality-capped historical enum is not global $2^{9}$ optimality;
uncalibrated physics parameters; equal OD weights;
any remaining \texttt{LIMIT\_REACHED} cells stay unresolved.
CI graph tests require the packed multiroute artifact (\texttt{multiroute-graph-v1}).

\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}
Under the frozen Moroccan panel and single-duty HOS screen, refrigerated eActros operational coverage rises from $9/14$ to $11/14$ with Benguerir and to $12/14$ with Benguerir+Larache under matched multi-route evaluation.
Threshold, infrastructure, and sensitivity results answer RQ1--RQ3 without claiming a new LRP solver or global optimization superiority.
