\title{Operational Feasibility and Charging Infrastructure Requirements for Battery-Electric Refrigerated Freight: A Scenario-Based Assessment of Moroccan Motorway Corridors}
\author{Ayoub Hanif}
\address{EMSI / PhD research, Casablanca, Morocco}
\begin{abstract}
We assess under which vehicle, infrastructure, and operating conditions battery-electric refrigerated heavy-duty trucks can feasibly serve selected Moroccan motorway freight missions.
A multi-resource label-setting solver with hours-of-service (HOS) resources enforced inside search is used as a research \emph{instrument}, not as a claimed algorithmic contribution.
On a frozen 14-mission panel with nine directional service-area candidates, operational coverage for an eActros~600 under cardinality-capped placement rises from $9/14$ with no new chargers to $11/14$ once a southbound Benguerir site is available; adding a second site (Larache) under fixed routing also reaches $11/14$, with no further certified multi-route gain at the B2 winner.
We do \emph{not} treat an ablation multi-on-C figure of $12/14$ as the B2 headline.
Research questions address (RQ1)~payload / departure-SOC thresholds, (RQ2)~which directional sites improve coverage versus site budget, and (RQ3)~sensitivity to routing flexibility, energy discretization~$q$, and physical assumptions.
Validated supporting findings include restored fixed$\subseteq$multi nesting after escalating \texttt{LIMIT\_REACHED} cells, coarse $q{=}5$ false infeasibility relative to $q\in\{2,1\}$, and documented physics reference checks (not field validation).
Claims are bound to Path~A reconciliation tables, Stage~C CSVs, and \texttt{SCIENTIFIC\_VALIDATION\_REPORT.md}.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:intro}
Electrifying refrigerated long-haul freight requires answering an operational question before algorithm novelty:
under which vehicle, infrastructure, and duty conditions can missions be served within a single duty period?
Recent TR-C work on e-truck charging and HOS~\cite{VidottePlaza2026TRC,Cheng2026TemperatureAware} motivates studying feasibility boundaries rather than competing on a new generic location--routing formulation.
This paper reports a scenario-based assessment of Moroccan motorway corridors.
The solver (MRLS with HOS-in-search, cardinality $\le 2$) is an instrument for that assessment.

\section{Research questions}
\label{sec:rq}
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{RQ1.} Which payload / departure-SOC / operating thresholds separate feasible from infeasible refrigerated eHDT missions?
\item \textbf{RQ2.} Which directional candidate sites improve operational coverage as the site budget grows from B0 to B1 to B2?
\item \textbf{RQ3.} How sensitive are conclusions to routing flexibility, energy discretization~$q$, and physical assumptions?
\end{enumerate}

\section{Methods}
\label{sec:methods}
\subsection{Instrument}
DEM-aware traction, auxiliaries, and an approximate refrigeration (UA--COP) load feed discrete DRIVE tables.
Directional ADM service-area candidates may be activated as chargers.
MRLS enforces continuous driving (qualifying break $\ge 45$~min from consecutive CHARGE+WAIT) and Art.~17 daily driving ($\le 9$~h) inside search.
Overnight Art.~16 daily-rest reset is \emph{not} implemented: missions that would require it are labeled \texttt{MULTI\_DAY\_OUT\_OF\_SCOPE} and excluded from served counts.
Independent schedule audit must agree with search break logic; energy-only coverage uses a separate \texttt{hos\_in\_search=False} solve so energy and ops can differ when HOS binds.

\subsection{Case design}
Fourteen equal-weight ODs~\cite{OpenStreetMapMoroccoMotorway,CopernicusDEMGLO30}; weather caches~\cite{OpenMeteoWeather};
nine grounded directional ADM sites; primary $q{=}2$~kWh with controlled $q\in\{5,2,1\}$ cells;
certification protocol $600$~s / $2\times10^{6}$ expansions with status $\in\{\texttt{OPTIMAL},\texttt{INFEASIBLE},\texttt{LIMIT\_REACHED}\}$.
Experimental design is frozen in \texttt{results/validation/path\_a/experimental\_design\_freeze.md}.

\subsection{Physics plausibility}
Flat-cruise traction for a representative refrigerated combination is order $1.34$~kWh/km versus a published eActros long-haul band $\sim 1.11$--$1.14$~kWh/km (different duty cycles).
Refrigeration setpoint, UA, and Wh accounting on DRIVE/CHARGE/WAIT are tabulated in
\texttt{results/validation/path\_a/physics\_plausibility.csv}.
We claim order-of-magnitude credibility, not field validation.

\section{Results}
\label{sec:results}

\subsection{RQ2 --- infrastructure ladder and critical sites}
Authoritative eActros operational coverage
(\texttt{results/validation/path\_a/coverage\_reconciliation.csv}):
B0 $9/14$; B1 fixed $C{=}10/14$ (Larache) and multi $E{=}11/14$ (Benguerir CA);
B2 fixed $C{=}11/14$ (Benguerir+Larache) and multi $E{=}11/14$ (Benguerir alone).
Ablation multi-on-C reaches $12/14$ via a \texttt{FLEX\_GAIN} on \texttt{casa\_marrakech}; that cell is \emph{not} the B2 manuscript headline.
Matched flexibility therefore shows little or zero certified gain at the B2 winner sites --- a legitimate finding, not a failure mode.
\texttt{LIMIT\_REACHED} is never equated with infeasibility; nesting claims use escalated $2\times10^{6}$ solves~\cite{OpenStreetMapMoroccoMotorway}.

\subsection{RQ1 --- operating thresholds}
With Benguerir CA installed, Casa$\rightarrow$Marrakech / Casa$\rightarrow$Agadir remain largely feasible for departure SOC~$\ge 0.6$ across $\{5.3,10,15\}$~t;
failures concentrate at SOC~$0.5$ with heavier payloads
(\texttt{results/validation/p6\_sensitivity/}).
These are model-scenario fractions, not calibrated real-world probabilities.

\subsection{RQ3 --- flexibility, discretization, physics}
Controlled Agadir$\rightarrow$Casablanca (15~t, SOC~$0.8$): $q{=}5$ is \texttt{INFEASIBLE} while $q\in\{2,1\}$ are \texttt{OPTIMAL} near reserve (min SOC $\approx 10.8\%$) --- coarse-grid false infeasibility.
Directionality (AC vs CA Benguerir access) unlocks selected disputed ODs under matched installs.
CdA/aux/weather sensitivities re-bake DRIVE tables before re-evaluation; physics reference checks are documented rather than claimed as OEM calibration.

\section{Discussion}
\label{sec:disc}
Infeasible-after-infrastructure and ``flexibility adds little'' are reportable outcomes for corridor planning:
they bound where charging investment and operating policies matter most.
Plaza et al.\ and temperature-aware TR-C work~\cite{VidottePlaza2026TRC,Cheng2026TemperatureAware} remain motivation for \emph{not} competing on algorithm novelty here.
Energy-only coverage can exceed HOS-ops when daily or continuous resources bind; dual evaluation keeps those metrics uncontaminated.

\section{Limitations}
\label{sec:limits}
Single duty day (no Art.~16 overnight reset); cardinality cap~$2$ with 45~s screening; uncalibrated physics parameters; equal OD weights; B1 Settat cell may remain unresolved \texttt{LIMIT\_REACHED} and is footnoted as best-among-resolved.
CI graph tests require the packed multiroute artifact (\texttt{GRAPH\_ARTIFACT\_URL}).

\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}
Under the frozen Moroccan panel and single-duty HOS screen, refrigerated eActros missions reach $11/14$ operational coverage once critical directional sites (notably Benguerir CA) are available; further B2 multi-route gain is not certified.
Threshold, infrastructure, and sensitivity results answer RQ1--RQ3 without claiming a new LRP solver or global optimality over $2^{9}$ subsets.
