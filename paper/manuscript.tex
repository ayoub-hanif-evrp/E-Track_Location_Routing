\begin{abstract}
We study joint charging-infrastructure placement and energy-constrained multi-route planning for refrigerated battery-electric heavy trucks on a \textbf{national} Moroccan motorway envelope spanning Tangier--Rabat--Casablanca--F\`es--Marrakech--Agadir.
The model couples an OSM/DEM physics stack, firm-ripe tomato refrigeration, multi-resource label setting (MRLS), and exhaustive site enumeration over direction-specific ADM service-area candidates.
Default CHARGE physics uses a conservative SOC-dependent CCS acceptance envelope
(\texttt{charging\_model=ccs\_soc\_taper\_research\_envelope}) --- a research assumption, not an OEM-measured curve.
\textbf{Operational coverage} requires energy feasibility under continuous replay \emph{and} d\'ecret~2-10-314 continuous-driving feasibility.
We embed continuous-driving time since the last qualifying break as an MRLS label resource (\emph{HOS-in-search}), so schedule feasibility is optimized rather than repaired post hoc.

Authoritative coverage numbers are Stage~C CSVs under \texttt{results/paper\_campaign/} (HOS-in-search; see \texttt{campaign\_manifest.json}).
Pre-HOS eActros B2 was E$=$F$=0.86$ vs C$=0.79$ with different directional Benguerir sites (placement--flexibility confound); Stage~C re-scores and identical-site ablation supersede those figures.
Payload$\times$SOC frontier confirms are q-grid sensitive: Agadir$\rightarrow$Casablanca at 15~t / SOC~$0.8$ flips from unserved at $q{=}5$ to eActros-served at $q{=}2$; retained flex advantages at SOC$\ge$80\% fall from 6 screen cells to 2 after $q{=}2$ confirmation (\texttt{survives\_q2\_flex}).
Method F is greedy over exhaustive enum scores unless \texttt{blind\_greedy\_vs\_enum.csv} shows an independent match.
Chargers are hypothetical; OD weights are equal research proxies.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:intro}

Battery-electric heavy-duty tractors are discussed for Moroccan long-distance refrigerated logistics.
Related TR-C work already combines charger location with routing and driver hours~\cite{VidottePlaza2026TRC} and studies temperature-aware siting~\cite{TemperatureAwareSiting2026TRC}.
Our contribution is a \emph{case-grounded} national Moroccan stack: DEM-complete OD physics, directional ADM access, refrigerated TRU loads~\cite{Bank2025IJR}, SOC CCS taper, and operational coverage under HOS-in-search.

\paragraph{Non-claims.}
We do not claim a new generic LRP theorem, OEM-calibrated charge curves, measured traffic weights, or certified 350~kW truck chargers at ADM sites~\cite{ADMServiceAreas}.

\section{Mathematical model (summary)}
\label{sec:model}

\subsection{Routing resources}
A label is $L=(i,t,E^{\mathrm{tr}},E^{\mathrm{tl}},c,\kappa,\delta)$, where $i$ is the node, $t$ discrete time, $E$ on-grid energies, $c$ en-route cost, $\kappa\in\{0,1\}$ charge eligibility, and $\delta$ is continuous-driving steps since the last qualifying break.
DRIVE accumulates $\delta$; expansions with $\delta+\Delta t>\bar\delta$ (4.5~h) are forbidden under HOS-in-search~\cite{MoroccoDecret210314}.
CHARGE/WAIT of duration $\ge$45~min at rest-capable stations reset $\delta=0$.
Dominance is same-$(i,t)$ Pareto over $(c,E,\kappa,\delta)$ with smaller $\delta$ weakly preferred.

\subsection{Placement}
For candidate sites $Y$ and budgets $B$, exhaustive enumeration maximizes equal-weight operational coverage over missions $M$, with secondary cost tie-breaks.
Method C optimizes fixed-route masks; E optimizes multi-route masks; F is greedy over \emph{precomputed} exhaustive subset scores (not a blind cheap greedy).

\section{Discussion and related work}
\label{sec:disc}
Compared with~\cite{VidottePlaza2026TRC}, we emphasize Moroccan DEM/ADM geometry, refrigerated loads, and numerical feasibility boundaries rather than a new hours-integrated MIP.
Equal weights and research taper limit external validity~\cite{Bank2025IJR}.
HOS-in-search lowers headline coverage relative to energy-only+pad because long ODs hit expansion limits; unresolved cells are labeled \texttt{LIMIT\_REACHED}, not physical impossibility.

\section{Limitations}
\label{sec:limits}
Site sets are frozen from pre-HOS exhaustive enum; full HOS re-enumeration remains optional (\texttt{--phase headline\_enum}).
Some long ODs remain \texttt{LIMIT\_REACHED} at 180--600~s; they are not counted as certified infeasible.
No observed eHDT trip sample; axle/HGV access unverified; eastern Oujda ODs excluded without invented attach.

\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}
The repository separates placement from flexibility, corrects q-grid contradictions, and optimizes continuous-driving inside MRLS so operational coverage means search-found energy+HOS feasibility under continuous replay audit.
Claims are tied to Stage~C artifacts and \texttt{campaign\_manifest.json}.
